When I remove one plasmid's capability, I want that one removed and nobody else's. And when a
plasmid grants the same kind of capability twice, I want two things I can remove separately.

Precise, durable and observable are three separate wants, not one. This is the precise one and
003 is the durable one. The observable one — being able to look at a cell's record and see for
myself that a capability was really removed — I recognise and am deliberately putting off: that it
is proven in the tests is enough for now.
